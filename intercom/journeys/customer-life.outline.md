# Intercom customer life

Author: `Codex`.

Dub leaves its Intercom client credentials unset. Cal.com ships the global app
disabled and requires an administrator to configure its keys. Their pinned
settings and evidence are under `optional` in `demand.json`. There are no demanded
customer acts, credentials, stored resources, waits or refresh calls. Every spec
operation answers the vendor-shaped gap; published examples are retained unchanged
and counted as unserved.
