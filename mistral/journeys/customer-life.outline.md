# Mistral customer life

Author: Contributor 94

Maya connects the built-in CRM provider with its console key, drafts and streams a follow-up,
asks the company tool and continues from its result, requests structured text and a short output budget.
An unissued credential is refused. Turns are scripts or labeled deterministic stubs; no model runs.
The stateless response has no list, delete or refresh path. Files, OCR, agents and model discovery remain outside demand.

A scripted capacity refusal is retried as a scripted follow-up. Reconnecting retains the application key.

Streaming also requests two short alternatives: both choice indices finish at the output budget, and usage charges the prompt once and both completions.
